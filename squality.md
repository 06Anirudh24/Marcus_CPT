## Example 1
### Question
In the context of "Les Misérables," written by Victor Hugo in 1862, what is the main setting of the novel?
### Answer
The main setting of "Les Misérables" is Paris, during 19th-century France, a period marked by social and political turmoil.

## Example 2
### Question
In the context of "Brave New World," written by Aldous Huxley in 1932, what is Soma, and what is its significance?
### Answer
In "Brave New World," Soma is a drug used to suppress discomfort and maintain social stability, symbolizing the dehumanizing effects of excessive control.

## Example 3
### Question
In the context of "1984," written by George Orwell in 1949, what is Newspeak, and why is it significant?
### Answer
Newspeak in "1984" is a language designed to restrict freedom of thought, serving as a tool for the Party to maintain control and suppress dissent.

## Example 4
### Question
In the context of "Moby-Dick," written by Herman Melville in 1851, what is the significance of the Pequod?
### Answer
In "Moby-Dick," the Pequod symbolizes humanity's conflict with nature and the perils of obsession, as its doomed journey reflects Captain Ahab’s tragic quest for revenge.

## Example 5
### Question
In the context of "The Great Gatsby," written by F. Scott Fitzgerald in 1925, what is the plot of the story?
### Answer
The plot of "The Great Gatsby" revolves around Jay Gatsby’s attempt to reunite with his former love, Daisy Buchanan, while exposing the corruption and emptiness of the American Dream in the 1920s.
