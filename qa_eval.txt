### Claim

- False
### Thought Process and Answer
Thought process: The feuding families in "Romeo and Juliet" are the Montagues and the Capulets, not the Bennets and Darcys. The Bennets and Darcys are characters from "Pride and Prejudice" by Jane Austen.
